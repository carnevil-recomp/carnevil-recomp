Controls
  5 or C ............ insert coin
  1 / 2 ............. player 1 / player 2 start
  9 ................. service credit
  - / = ............. volume down / up
  mouse ............. aim the light gun (point off the picture to reload)
  left click / Ctrl . trigger
  Space ............. pump

Gamepads (first pad player 1, second pad player 2; moving the mouse gives player 1 back to it)
  left stick / d-pad  aim
  A or right trigger  trigger
  B or left trigger   pump
  Y or right shoulder reload
  Start / Menu ...... start
  Back / Options .... coin

Options (after the image path)
  --crosshair 0    no crosshairs (the cabinet had none; the port draws them like MAME)
  --hires          smooth window-resolution picture instead of the cabinet's 400x256 one
  --violence 0..3  0 none, 1 green blood, 2 red blood, 3 full gore (default); it is saved
  --adjust N=V     any operator adjustment by its number in the game's ADJUST.FMT
